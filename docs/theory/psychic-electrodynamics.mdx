# Psychic Electrodynamics

Psychic Electrodynamics provides the energy model for how symbolic tension operates within the Woven Map.

## The Charge Model

- **Potential Difference** — Tension between planetary positions creates symbolic voltage.
- **Current** — Active transits carry charge through the system.
- **Resistance** — Hard aspects create impedance where pressure builds.
- **Discharge** — Recognition (ping) releases accumulated tension.

## Circuit Patterns

### Closed Circuits
When tension has no outlet, pressure builds. Maps to suppressed vectors in the Vector Architecture.

### Open Circuits
When tension flows freely, pressure dissipates. Maps to released vectors.

### Short Circuits
When pressure discharges suddenly — breakthroughs, crises, revelations — the system experiences rapid state change.

## Clinical Application

The electrodynamic model frames psychological experience in non-pathologizing terms. "Anxiety" becomes "accumulated charge seeking discharge." "Breakthrough" becomes "circuit completion."
