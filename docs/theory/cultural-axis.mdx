# The Cultural Axis

The Cultural Axis explores how collective, societal-level symbolism intersects with individual natal charts.

## Collective vs. Individual

- **Individual Chart** — Personal geometry, unique to the native.
- **Collective Transits** — Planetary movements affecting everyone simultaneously.
- **Cultural Axis** — Where the two intersect, creating shared symbolic climates.

## The Surprise

Collective transits often activate personal chart points in ways that feel uniquely intimate — even though the transit is universal. This is resonance: the same geometry can speak both collectively and personally.

## Application

- **Timing Analysis** — When personal transits align with collective ones, pressure amplifies.
- **Generational Patterns** — Outer planet placements create shared archetypal themes.
- **Cultural Moments** — Major conjunctions create collective symbolic weather.

## Constraints

- Collective transits never override personal agency.
- The framework distinguishes between personal resonance and collective climate.
