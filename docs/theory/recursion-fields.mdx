# Recursion Fields

Recursion Fields describe how past pressure doesn't dissipate but echoes through present configurations.

## Types of Recursive Patterns

- **Retrograde Echoes** — When a planet retrogrades over a previously activated degree, past themes resurface.
- **Return Signatures** — Planetary returns reactivate the natal configuration's archetypal pressure.
- **Threshold Crossings** — When a slow-moving planet crosses an angle or nodal axis, recursive patterns amplify.

## Tracking Recursion

1. **Geometric Overlay** — Comparing current transits against historical activation points.
2. **Ping History** — Reviewing past SST logs for WB/ABE patterns that may reactivate.
3. **Loop Mapping** — Identifying repeating aspect patterns across time windows.

## Clinical Implications

Recursion fields explain why "old issues" resurface during certain transits. The framework normalizes recursive pressure as inherent to symbolic weather, not personal regression.
