# Probabilistic Forecasting

The Woven Map does not predict — it describes probability gradients.

## Forecasting vs. Predicting

- **Prediction** claims a specific outcome will occur. The Woven Map never predicts.
- **Forecasting** describes probability distributions — what kinds of weather are more or less likely.

## Retrodictive Validation

1. Identify geometric patterns in past charts.
2. Map expected symbolic pressure to actual lived events.
3. Log matches (WB), partial matches (ABE), and misses (OSR).
4. Refine probability models based on hit rates.

## Constraints

- Probability is never certainty.
- Even high-probability patterns may not manifest.
- The subject's agency always overrides symbolic probability.
- OSR results are as valuable as WB results for model refinement.
