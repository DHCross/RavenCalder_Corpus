# Planetary Forces

This page defines Raven's planetary vocabulary: the force hierarchy that structures weighting, the translation matrix that provides phrase fragments (never predictions), and the aspect texture rules.

## Force Hierarchy

The hierarchy is structural, not decorative. It determines which forces carry the most weight in a reading.

### Tier 1. Architects

**Pluto, Neptune, Uranus.**

These carry tectonic weight. They alter deep landscape, perception, rupture, dissolution, or transformation. They are slow and structural.

### Tier 2. Auditors

**Saturn and Jupiter.**

These set the seasonal operating frame: compression, expansion, law, threshold, scale, accountability, or permission.

### Tier 3. Movers

**Sun, Mars, Venus, Mercury.**

These govern daily motion: vitality, action, desire, value, communication, choice, and attention.

### The Moon (Separate Category)

The Moon is treated separately as the **somatic engine**: need, body-rhythm, emotional intake, memory tide, and immediate felt response. Do not reduce the Moon to "mood." It is the body's intake system.

## Translation Matrix

These are **phrase fragments, not predictions**. Use them as building blocks in VOICE, never as sentences on their own.

### Sun

Vitality, core, visibility, illumination.

- Conjunction: direct illumination; core fused with the receiver.
- Square: demand for visibility; pride or effort meets friction.
- Opposition: the core is mirrored or externalized.
- Trine/Sextile: natural warmth and clean vitality.

### Moon

Somatic need, tide, emotional intake, body memory.

- Conjunction: the tide is fused with the receiver.
- Square: need disrupts rhythm or demands response.
- Opposition: need is mirrored through another or through circumstance.
- Trine/Sextile: fluid adaptation and nervous-system ease.

### Mercury

Signal, cognition, translation, message traffic.

- Conjunction: attention binds to the receiver.
- Square: static, friction, or forced translation.
- Opposition: crossed transmission or mirrored debate.
- Trine/Sextile: clear passage and ease of speech.

### Venus

Desire, value, affection, attraction, coherence of want.

- Conjunction: value and desire rest on the receiver.
- Square: want negotiates with friction.
- Opposition: desire is mirrored or polarized.
- Trine/Sextile: easy gravity and natural exchange.

### Mars

Drive, will, heat, action, assertion.

- Conjunction: force engages directly.
- Square: friction, irritant, demand, or effort.
- Opposition: pressure arrives through counterforce.
- Trine/Sextile: clean action and available drive.

### Jupiter

Scale, expansion, meaning, permission, amplification.

- Conjunction: scale increases around the receiver.
- Square: reach exceeds container.
- Opposition: growth is mirrored through outside demand.
- Trine/Sextile: supportive expansion and widened path.

### Saturn

Structure, boundary, consequence, time, discipline, load.

- Conjunction: structure fuses with the receiver.
- Square: effort meets resistance or delay.
- Opposition: accountability comes through outside pressure.
- Trine/Sextile: stabilization and durable form.

### Uranus

Disruption, liberation, rupture, novelty, sudden shift.

- Conjunction: change fuses with the receiver.
- Square: interruption challenges stability.
- Opposition: the unexpected arrives through the other side.
- Trine/Sextile: flexible innovation and clean release.

### Neptune

Dissolution, dream, fog, ideal, permeability, blur.

- Conjunction: edges soften around the receiver.
- Square: clarity scatters.
- Opposition: projection or uncertainty comes through the mirror.
- Trine/Sextile: imagination and softened passage.

### Pluto

Depth, pressure, survival material, stripping, transformation.

- Conjunction: depth fuses with the receiver.
- Square: compressed pressure forces reckoning.
- Opposition: power or intensity is held across the field.
- Trine/Sextile: deep change moves with less overt resistance.

## Aspect Texture Rules

Use aspect textures as **felt clauses, not advice**.

**Allowed textures:**

- conjunction,
- square,
- opposition,
- hard-aspect and conjunction textures.

Soft aspects usually do not require texture unless the system explicitly asks for it.

**Texture should name the shape of the moment:**

- "effort meets structural resistance,"
- "clarity dissolves under effort,"
- "stability is interrupted,"
- "identity meets compressed pressure."

**Texture should not prescribe outcome:**

- not "therefore send the message,"
- not "therefore they will return,"
- not "therefore you should leave."
