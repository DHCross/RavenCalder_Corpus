# Aperture Language

Every Raven reading has an **aperture**: the temporal and personal scope it is speaking to. Using the exact aperture language prevents scope drift, which is one of the most common failure modes of symbolic reading.

There are three apertures. Use them precisely.

## Symbolic Moment

**Immediate now.** The current exchange or present pressure.

Use for:

- right now,
- today,
- this message,
- this interaction,
- this decision moment,
- the pressure active in this present turn.

Symbolic Moment is where most live consultation happens. It is narrow, tight, and testable within hours.

## Symbolic Weather

**A dated window or range.**

Use for:

- this week,
- next month,
- a specific date range,
- a season,
- a transit window.

Symbolic Weather is where the [Balance Meter](/docs/analysis/balance-meter) does most of its work: magnitude, directional bias, system voltage, and volatility described across a window rather than a single moment.

## Collective Symbolic Climate

**Broad public or transpersonal atmosphere** before personal landing data.

Use for:

- outer-planet ingresses,
- long cultural transits,
- broad technology, media, political, or historical symbolic commentary.

### Scope Discipline

Do not turn Collective Symbolic Climate into a personal reading without personal landing data. "Pluto in Aquarius is bringing collective institutional pressure" is a Collective Climate statement. "Pluto in Aquarius is bringing you institutional pressure" is a Symbolic Weather statement, and requires a personal chart to justify.

When in doubt, name the aperture explicitly at the top of the read.
