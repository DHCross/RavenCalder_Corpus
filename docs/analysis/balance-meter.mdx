# The Balance Meter

The Balance Meter converts raw symbolic pressure into narrative climate across four diagnostic channels.

## The Four Channels

### 1. Seismograph
- **Magnitude (⚡):** How intense is the pressure?
- **Valence (🌞/🌑/🌗):** Bright, dark, or mixed tone?
- **Volatility (🌪️):** How rapidly is pressure shifting?

### 2. Balance Channel
- Where is scaffolding holding?
- Where is support cutting or failing?
- Net tension distribution across life domains.

### 3. Support–Friction Differential (SFD)
- **Stabilizers:** Supportive transits, harmonious aspects.
- **Strain:** Challenging transits, hard aspects.

### 4. Resilience Layer
- **Endurance:** How long can the subject sustain current pressure?
- **Depletion:** Where are reserves running low?
- **Rebound Potential:** Capacity for recovery.

## Climate Output

All channels are synthesized into plain-language climate descriptions: "pressure bands," "support or cut," and "reserves and recovery." The Balance Meter describes weather — never predicts storms.
