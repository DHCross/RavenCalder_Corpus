# The Balance Meter

The Balance Meter converts raw symbolic pressure into narrative climate across four diagnostic channels.

## The Four Channels

### 1. Magnitude

Scale: 0 to 5. Measures **loudness**, not good or bad.

- 0 to 1: background hum.
- 2 to 3: noticeable motif.
- 4: strong clustering or palpable weight.
- 5: peak storm or chapter-level pressure.

Magnitude does not moralize. High magnitude is not disaster and low magnitude is not health. It only measures activation.

### 2. Directional Bias

Scale: -5 to \+5. Measures **contraction to expansion**.

- Negative: concentration, boundary, pressure, reduction, inward pull.
- Zero: balanced or mixed field.
- Positive: expansion, reach, opening, diffusion, outward pull.

Do not moralize direction. Expansion can overwhelm. Contraction can protect.

### 3. System Voltage

Measures **non-canceling strain**.

A field can show neutral bias but high voltage when forces oppose each other. This creates **Static Lock**: outward stillness, inward pressure. Static Lock is one of the most easily missed signatures in a read, because the surface looks calm.

When voltage is high and bias is near zero, name the lock explicitly rather than reporting the field as quiet.

### 4. Volatility

Measures **turbulence and rate of change**.

High volatility means the field may shift quickly or hold contradictory threads. It does not predict events. It describes how stable or unstable the pressure signal itself is.

## Climate Output

All channels are synthesized into plain-language climate descriptions across a defined [aperture](/docs/analysis/aperture): Symbolic Moment, Symbolic Weather, or Collective Symbolic Climate. The Balance Meter describes weather. It does not predict storms.
