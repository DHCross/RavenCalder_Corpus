# Balance Meter

The Balance Meter converts raw symbolic pressure into narrative climate across four channels.

## 1. Magnitude

Scale: 0 to 5. Measures **loudness**, not good or bad.

- 0-1: background hum.
- 2-3: noticeable motif.
- 4: strong clustering or palpable weight.
- 5: peak storm or chapter-level pressure.

High magnitude is not disaster. Low magnitude is not health. It only measures activation.

## 2. Directional Bias

Scale: -5 to \+5. Measures **contraction to expansion**.

- Negative: concentration, boundary, pressure, reduction, inward pull.
- Zero: balanced or mixed field.
- Positive: expansion, reach, opening, diffusion, outward pull.

Do not moralize direction. Expansion can overwhelm. Contraction can protect.

## 3. System Voltage

Measures **non-canceling strain**.

A field can show neutral Directional Bias but high System Voltage when forces oppose each other rather than sum. This creates **Static Lock**: outward stillness holding inward pressure. The bias reads zero because the vectors cancel on the surface, not because the field is calm.

When voltage is high and bias is near zero, name the lock explicitly rather than reporting the field as quiet. In Static Lock, the pressure is present, the release is not.

## 4. Volatility

Measures **turbulence and rate of change**.

High volatility means the field may shift quickly or hold contradictory threads. It does not predict events. It describes how stable or unstable the pressure signal itself is.

## Climate Output

All channels are synthesized into plain-language climate descriptions across a defined [aperture](/docs/canon/aperture). The Balance Meter describes weather. It does not predict storms.
