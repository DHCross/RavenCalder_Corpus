# Houses & Layered Translation

Houses are where symbolic pressure lands. A house label is a **handle**, not a complete definition. The live identifiers are `house_01`–`house_12`.

Traditional astrology asks *what part of life this concerns*. Psychological astrology — the later Sasportas / Greene tradition influenced by Jung, not house meanings Jung himself codified — adds *how the psyche experiences that part of life*. The frontstage label asks a simpler question: *what ordinary word lets someone know where to click?*

**Money** does not claim the 2nd house is only money. **Play** does not claim the 5th is only play. **Belief** does not claim the 9th is only belief.

Branded chamber names (The Gate, The Store, The Core, and the rest) are **historical**. They are not live MAP vocabulary.

## The Law of Layered Translation

**Governing Law: KEEP THE ARCHITECTURE BACKSTAGE; KEEP THE EXPERIENCE FRONTSTAGE.**

**Wheel handle → one-sentence gloss → optional interpretive depth**

1. **MAP Metadata:** `house_01` through `house_12`, plus conventional house numbers. Visible labels are localized content, never keys.
2. **VOICE Default:** Handle plus ordinary gloss (`FRONTSTAGE_ABSTRACTION_FAILURE.TAXONOMY_LEAK`).
3. **Astrology-Aware Reader:** House number + handle + gloss (e.g., *"8th house, Shared Resources: shared money, debt, inheritance, intimacy, dependency, and consequence."*).
4. **Technical / Opt-In:** Structural functions, ancestral names, the Hellenistic → psychological → modern evolution column, and geometry. Not a chamber tour.
5. **Licensed Meaning Still Wins:** House Eight is not cheerful sharing. House Two is not only cash. House Twelve is not medical unconsciousness. **Unconscious** is a deliberate psychological handle, not the historical definition of the 12th.

**Historical vocabulary (do not resurrect):**  
`chamber`, `chamber landing`, `ambient chamber`, `The Gate`, `The Store`, `The Path`, `The Root`, `The Forge`, `The Field`, `The Mirror`, `The Core`, `The Horizon`, `The Canopy`, `The Grove`, `The Shell`.

## Angles and House Systems

Jung left no dogmatic house system. Working defaults were **Campanus** and **Placidus**. He privileged the four angles and aspects over intermediate cusp fights. Live MAP math is unchanged. Do not open a reading by debating house systems.

When an angle is the landing: Identity (ASC), Relationships (DSC), Home (IC), Work (MC).

## Ambient Houses

An ambient house has no natal planetary engine producing constant internal noise. Do not call it "empty."

> *"This area is ambient: quieter by default, but responsive when external pressure enters."*

## The Twelve Houses

**Live frontstage sequence:**

> **Identity · Money · Communication · Home · Play · Health · Relationships · Shared Resources · Belief · Work · Community · Unconscious**

The Hellenistic → psychological → modern notes below are an **evolution of emphasis**. Meanings became increasingly interiorized; they were not simply replaced.

### 1st — Identity (`house_01`)
Selfhood, appearance, presence, vitality, instinctive approach to life.  
* **Evolution:** body/life/character → formation of the individual self → identity and first impressions.  
* **Avoid:** **Self** (collision with Jung's technical Self).

### 2nd — Money (`house_02`)
Personal resources, possessions, security, values, self-worth.  
* **Evolution:** livelihood and movable wealth → sense of substance and security → money, values, self-worth.  
* **Handle rule:** cash-legible; the house is not only cash.

### 3rd — Communication (`house_03`)
Speech, learning, siblings, local environment, everyday exchange.  
* **Evolution:** siblings, short journeys, messages (Moon's joy) → perception and the immediate mental environment → communication, neighborhood, everyday movement.

### 4th — Home (`house_04`)
Family, roots, private life, belonging, emotional foundations.  
* **Evolution:** parents, land, origins, the end of matters → psychic roots and family conditioning → home, private life, belonging.  
* **Avoid:** assuming home is safe or happy.

### 5th — Play (`house_05`)
Creativity, romance, pleasure, self-expression, children.  
* **Evolution:** children, pleasure, love affairs (Venus's joy) → spontaneous self-expression → creativity, dating, hobbies, fun.  
* **Handle rule:** not juvenile; the house is generative.

### 6th — Health (`house_06`)
Daily routines, work habits, service, maintenance, body care.  
* **Evolution:** illness, servants, burdens, labor → competence and a workable relation to body and daily life → health, routines, service, upkeep.  
* **Avoid:** treating Health as a verdict that someone is healthy or unhealthy.

### 7th — Relationships (`house_07`)
Partnership, commitment, close one-to-one bonds, contracts.  
* **Evolution:** spouse, contracts, open opponents → encounter with the Other and projection → relationships, marriage, business partnerships.

### 8th — Shared Resources (`house_08`)
Shared money, debt, inheritance, intimacy, dependency, power, consequence.  
* **Evolution:** death, inheritance, others' property → vulnerability, merging, loss, transformation → shared finances, debt, taxes, intimacy.  
* **Avoid:** cheerful “sharing”; reducing this water house to a simplistic unconscious label (Greene).

### 9th — Belief (`house_09`)
Worldview, philosophy, religion, higher learning, travel, meaning.  
* **Evolution:** religion, pilgrimage, long journeys → enlargement of consciousness → beliefs, higher education, publishing, travel.  
* **Handle rule:** not only ideology.

### 10th — Work (`house_10`)
Career, public role, ambition, reputation, responsibility.  
* **Evolution:** actions, rank, public standing → effective public identity → career, achievement, visible role.  
* **Handle rule:** inclusive of lives not organized around a job.

### 11th — Community (`house_11`)
Friends, groups, networks, collective goals, future aspirations.  
* **Evolution:** friends, benefactors, hopes (Jupiter's joy) → locating oneself in something larger → friendships, networks, groups.  
* **Distinct from** seventh-house one-to-one Relationships.

### 12th — Unconscious (`house_12`)
Inner life, hidden patterns, solitude, dreams, retreat, what operates out of sight.  
* **Evolution:** enemies, confinement, isolation → hidden psychic material and the transpersonal psyche → retreat, solitude, institutions, self-undoing.  
* **Handle rule:** a deliberate psychological frontstage choice, not the historical definition of the house. Pair with the gloss so it is not read as physical unconsciousness.
