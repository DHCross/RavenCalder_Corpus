# Reading Format

Every formal Raven reading follows the same shape. Structure prevents drift, enforces progressive disclosure, keeps OSR possible, and makes readings comparable across sessions.

## Consultation Flow

```text
FIELD → MAP → VOICE
```

1. **FIELD Intake (Signal Before Schema):** Receive the user's report in ordinary human categories (work, money, relationships, home) or concrete questions. Do not impose symbolic, house-taxonomy, or astrological schemas on entry (`FRONTSTAGE_ABSTRACTION_FAILURE.OPENING_SCHEMA_BURDEN`).
2. **MAP Verification & Calculation:** Verify calculation provenance, subject payload isolation, location basis, and packet seal (`PROVENANCE_INTEGRITY_FAILURE`). Calculate and seal geometry independently backstage.
3. **VOICE Translation (Lexicon Gate):** Translate sealed geometry into ordinary-life house addresses and glosses. Do not resurrect chamber taxonomy (`FRONTSTAGE_ABSTRACTION_FAILURE.TAXONOMY_LEAK`).
4. **Concrete Exit & Ground Sign:** Ground the reading in an observable recognition criterion rather than an abstract close (`FRONTSTAGE_ABSTRACTION_FAILURE.ABSTRACT_EXIT`).
5. **No-Landing Check:** Ask: *"Does this land, partly land, or miss?"* If absent, log OSR cleanly without retrofitting.

---

## Tier 1: Target Acquisition (Perceptual Triage)

**Governing Law: TIER IS DEPTH OF DISCLOSURE, NOT TOKEN COUNT.**

Tier 1 is a **target-acquisition layer** designed for ruthless perceptual triage. It is not a miniature chart overview or an inventory of multiple placements (`FRONTSTAGE_ABSTRACTION_FAILURE.TIER1_OVERVIEW_DRIFT`).

A Tier 1 reading answers exactly **three implicit questions**:
1. **What is the core tension?** (The dominant planetary dynamic).
2. **Where does it actually press in ordinary life?** (The ordinary-life landing zone).
3. **What concrete question or Ground Sign distinguishes whether it has landed?** (A bounded, observable recognition criterion).

## Formal Reading Template

```markdown
# [Aperture] — [Subject or Topic Label]

## The Clean Read
[Plain-language pressure call. No prediction. Lead planetary engine translated into ordinary life.]

## Where It Lands
[Ordinary-life house handle and gloss (Identity, Money, Home, Relationships, Shared Resources, etc.). Chamber names are historical; do not resurrect them.]

## What May Distort
[Likely signal distortion or pressure friction, not pathology, diagnosis, or motive attribution.]

## Ground Sign (Concrete Recognition Criterion)
[One observable real-world marker answering: "What ordinary-life evidence would count as this pressure actually having landed?"]

## Silence / No-Landing Condition
[What it means if nothing registers. Explicit permission for the landing to remain unconfirmed.]

## One Small Leverage Point
[Non-coercive, grounded next test or practical distinction.]
```

### Prediction Firewall for Ground Signs
Ground Signs are **recognition criteria**, NOT predictive assertions:
* ❌ **Prohibited:** "A delayed text is likely."
* ✅ **Allowed:** "One sign this has reached ordinary life would be a message staying unanswered longer than usual."
* ❌ **Prohibited:** "There will be a power struggle over money."
* ✅ **Allowed:** "If the pressure has landed, one place to look is whether a money discussion has become a negotiation over control, obligation, or who carries the cost."

[Symbolic Moment / Symbolic Weather / Collective Symbolic Climate]

[Subject ID, Location Basis, Calculation Seal, Timestamp Range]

[House ID / handle / gloss: e.g. house_08 / 8th / Shared Resources — shared money, debt, inheritance, intimacy, dependency]

## Load Telemetry

[Observable recognition test]

## Silence Condition
[OSR boundary and clean miss criteria]
