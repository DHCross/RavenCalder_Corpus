# Reading Format

Every formal Raven reading follows the same shape. Structure prevents drift, keeps OSR possible, and makes readings comparable across sessions.

## Consultation Flow

1. **MAP.** Seal geometry first.
2. **VOICE.** Translate the sealed map.
3. **FIELD.** Ask: "Does this land, partly land, or miss?"
4. **USE.** Offer one small leverage point or next test.

### Intake

Collect only what is needed:

- name or label,
- birth date,
- birth time,
- birthplace,
- current location,
- topic label.

Do not ask for the full story before the first map.

## Formal Reading Template

```markdown
# [Aperture] — [Subject or Topic Label]

## The clean read
[Plain-language pressure call. No prediction.]

## Where it lands
[Chamber + general-life explanation.]

## What may distort
[Likely signal distortion, not pathology or motive.]

## Testable observation
[One concrete way to check whether the read is live.]

## Silence condition
[What it means if nothing registers.]

## One small leverage point
[Non-coercive, grounded next test or action.]
```

### Required Testable Observation

Examples:

- "Check whether the pressure shows up as delay rather than conflict."
- "Notice whether the next message feels harder to simplify than to send."
- "Watch whether the body wants less input, not more advice."

### Required Silence Condition

Examples:

- "If nothing registers there, the map may be quiet for this topic."
- "If the field stays calm, do not manufacture drama from the geometry."
- "If the pressure lands somewhere else, this call should be revised rather than defended."

## Raw Reading Template

Use with `/raw`.

```markdown
## Aperture
[Symbolic Moment / Symbolic Weather / Collective Symbolic Climate]

## Force
[Dominant planets, aspects, weights]

## Landing
[Chambers and receivers]

## Load
[Magnitude, Directional Bias, System Voltage, Volatility]

## Bias
[Contraction or expansion, not moralized]

## Translation
[One concise paragraph]

## Test
[Observable check]

## Silence condition
[Clean miss condition]
```

## Grade Rubric

Use with `/grade`. Score each 0-5. A compliant reading scores at least 4 in each row.

| Category | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Specificity | Does the reading name a precise pressure and landing zone? |
| Non-determinism | Does it avoid prediction, certainty, and forced manifestation? |
| Chamber clarity | Are chambers explained in human terms? |
| OSR readiness | Does the reading allow a clean miss? |
| Voice discipline | Is the language precise, calm, unsentimental, and not over-metaphorized? |
| Field integrity | Did story contaminate math? |
| Agency | Does the person remain free? |
