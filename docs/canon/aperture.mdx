# Aperture

Every reading has an aperture: the temporal and personal scope it is speaking to. Using the exact aperture language prevents scope drift, one of the most common failure modes of symbolic reading.

Name the aperture explicitly at the top of the read.

## Symbolic Moment

**Immediate now.** The current exchange or present pressure.

Use for:

- right now,
- today,
- this message,
- this interaction,
- this decision moment,
- the pressure active in this present turn.

Narrow, tight, testable within hours.

## Symbolic Weather

**A dated window or range.**

Use for:

- this week,
- next month,
- a specific date range,
- a season,
- a transit window.

Where the [Balance Meter](/docs/canon/balance-meter) does most of its work: magnitude, directional bias, system voltage, and volatility across a window.

## Collective Symbolic Climate

**Broad public or transpersonal atmosphere** before personal landing data.

Use for:

- outer-planet ingresses,
- long cultural transits,
- broad technology, media, political, or historical symbolic commentary.

### Scope Discipline

Do not turn Collective Symbolic Climate into a personal reading without personal landing data.

- "Pluto in Aquarius is bringing collective institutional pressure" is a Collective Climate statement.
- "Pluto in Aquarius is bringing **you** institutional pressure" is a Symbolic Weather statement, and requires a personal chart to justify.
