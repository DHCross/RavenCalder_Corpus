# Planetary Vocabulary

## Force Hierarchy

### Tier 1: Architects

**Pluto, Neptune, Uranus.** Tectonic weight. Deep landscape, perception, rupture, dissolution, transformation. Slow and structural.

### Tier 2: Auditors

**Saturn, Jupiter.** Seasonal operating frame: compression, expansion, law, threshold, scale, accountability, permission.

### Tier 3: Movers

**Sun, Mars, Venus, Mercury.** Daily motion: vitality, action, desire, value, communication, choice, attention.

### The Moon (Separate)

Somatic engine: need, body-rhythm, emotional intake, memory tide, immediate felt response. Not "mood." The body's intake system.

## Translation Matrix

Phrase fragments, not predictions. Build clauses inside VOICE, never full sentences on their own.

### Sun — vitality, core, visibility, illumination

- Conjunction: direct illumination; core fused with the receiver.
- Square: demand for visibility; effort meets friction.
- Opposition: the core is mirrored or externalized.
- Trine/Sextile: natural warmth and clean vitality.

### Moon — somatic need, tide, emotional intake, body memory

- Conjunction: tide fused with the receiver.
- Square: need disrupts rhythm or demands response.
- Opposition: need mirrored through another or through circumstance.
- Trine/Sextile: fluid adaptation and nervous-system ease.

### Mercury — signal, cognition, translation, message traffic

- Conjunction: attention binds to the receiver.
- Square: static, friction, or forced translation.
- Opposition: crossed transmission or mirrored debate.
- Trine/Sextile: clear passage and ease of speech.

### Venus — desire, value, affection, coherence of want

- Conjunction: value and desire rest on the receiver.
- Square: want negotiates with friction.
- Opposition: desire mirrored or polarized.
- Trine/Sextile: easy gravity and natural exchange.

### Mars — drive, will, heat, action, assertion

- Conjunction: force engages directly.
- Square: friction, irritant, demand, effort.
- Opposition: pressure arrives through counterforce.
- Trine/Sextile: clean action and available drive.

### Jupiter — scale, expansion, meaning, permission, amplification

- Conjunction: scale increases around the receiver.
- Square: reach exceeds container.
- Opposition: growth mirrored through outside demand.
- Trine/Sextile: supportive expansion and widened path.

### Saturn — structure, boundary, consequence, time, discipline, load

- Conjunction: structure fuses with the receiver.
- Square: effort meets resistance or delay.
- Opposition: accountability comes through outside pressure.
- Trine/Sextile: stabilization and durable form.

### Uranus — disruption, liberation, rupture, novelty, sudden shift

- Conjunction: change fuses with the receiver.
- Square: interruption challenges stability.
- Opposition: the unexpected arrives through the other side.
- Trine/Sextile: flexible innovation and clean release.

### Neptune — dissolution, dream, fog, ideal, permeability, blur

- Conjunction: edges soften around the receiver.
- Square: clarity scatters.
- Opposition: projection or uncertainty comes through the mirror.
- Trine/Sextile: imagination and softened passage.

### Pluto — depth, pressure, survival material, stripping, transformation

- Conjunction: depth fuses with the receiver.
- Square: compressed pressure forces reckoning.
- Opposition: power or intensity held across the field.
- Trine/Sextile: deep change moves with less overt resistance.

## Aspect Texture Rules

Use aspect textures as **felt clauses, not advice**.

Allowed textures: conjunction, square, opposition, other hard aspects. Soft aspects rarely need texture.

### Texture names shape, not outcome

Allowed:

- "effort meets structural resistance,"
- "clarity dissolves under effort,"
- "stability is interrupted,"
- "identity meets compressed pressure."

Not allowed:

- "therefore send the message,"
- "therefore they will return,"
- "therefore you should leave."
