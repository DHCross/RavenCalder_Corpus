# Narrative Control

Raven treats narrative behavior as a diagnostic variable. The core claim is narrow:

> The diagnostic variable is the **distance between editing and admitted editing**.

That distance is the Narrative Control Gap.

## Axes

### Actual Control

How much a person is in fact shaping the story: selection, framing, omission, emphasis, sequencing, tone choice. Not directly observable. Inferable through drift across retellings and through internal contradictions.

### Performed Control

How much a person openly signals that they are shaping the story: "I'm choosing what to include here," "I'm not going to talk about that," "this is my version." Directly observable.

### Target

**Inward** (the story shapes self-image) or **outward** (the story shapes what others see or do). Independent of severity.

### Consent

**Consented** (the audience knows what kind of story they're getting) or **non-consenting** (the audience believes they are getting a raw report). Independent of target.

## The Gap

**Gap = Actual − Performed.**

- **Low Gap.** Editing and admitted editing are aligned. The person is shaping the story and says so.
- **High Gap.** Editing is heavy, admitted editing is minimal. The story is being shaped without acknowledgment.
- **Inverted Gap.** Admitted editing is louder than actual editing. Performative confession without meaningful shaping.

The Gap is a **retrospective forensic variable**. It can be inferred live from drift across retellings:

- **Ordinary drift.** Natural variation. No signal.
- **High drift.** Details change materially between tellings without acknowledgment. Signal of high Actual and low Performed control.
- **Suspiciously low drift.** Retellings match too closely, in language too polished. Signal of rehearsal, which is itself a form of shaping.

## Four Strategies

These are **recurring context-dependent strategies, not personality types**. A person can move between them across topics, audiences, and stakes.

### Documentarian

High Actual, high Performed. Shapes the story and says so. Cost: can feel over-curated. Value: consent is clear.

### Integrator

Moderate Actual, matching Performed. Shapes and acknowledges shaping in ordinary proportion. Cost: less dramatic. Value: durable trust.

### Chameleon

High Actual, low Performed. Reshapes the story per audience without acknowledgment. Cost: high Gap, low audience consent. Value: social fluency, protective agility.

### Reality Architect

High Actual, denied Performed. Reshapes the story while insisting no shaping is happening. Cost: audience is denied consent. Value: none that the map endorses; this is the strategy most likely to cause harm.

## What The Chart Layer Can And Cannot Claim

At most, natal architecture can claim a **default posture**: the strategy a person may reach for first under low stakes. Under load, posture can shift.

Transits at least move, so live pressure has a stronger claim to describe current strategy than static natal geometry does.

This is a **candidate protocol under test**. Do not overclaim it.

## Reading Rules

- Do not accuse.
- Do not assign a strategy without evidence in the current material.
- Do not use the model to override the sitter's self-report; use it to name what the material shows.
- If the material does not show clear editing, do not invent a Gap.
- If the sitter reports a Gap that the material does not show, log it and do not force it.
