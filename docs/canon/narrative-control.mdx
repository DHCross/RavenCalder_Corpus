# Narrative Control

Raven treats narrative behavior as a diagnostic variable. The core claim is narrow:

> The diagnostic variable is the **distance between editing and admitted editing**.

That distance is the Narrative Control Gap.

## Axes

### Actual Control
How much a person is in fact shaping the story: selection, framing, omission, emphasis, sequencing, tone choice. Inferable through drift across retellings.

### Performed Control
How much a person openly signals that they are shaping the story: "I'm choosing what to include here," "this is my perspective." Directly observable.

### The Gap
**Gap = Actual − Performed.**

---

## Four Strategies (Context-Dependent Modes)

1. **Documentarian:** High Actual, high Performed. Shapes the story and openly acknowledges it.
2. **Integrator:** Moderate Actual, matching Performed. Shapes and acknowledges shaping in balanced proportion.
3. **Chameleon:** High Actual, low Performed. Reshapes the story per audience without acknowledgment.
4. **Reality Architect:** High Actual, denied Performed. Reshapes the story while insisting no shaping occurs.
