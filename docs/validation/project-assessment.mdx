Across the sessions in this project, the strongest feature is not that the readings always hit. It is that they have become progressively better at making clean, constrained claims that can survive comparison with reality.

## Recurring Strengths

### Structural specificity

The better readings do more than name a mood. They identify a kind of pressure, a landing zone, and often a mechanism: communication, shared load, public responsibility, home/root pressure, creation, maintenance, direct relationship contact. That is much harder to retrofit than "change" or "transformation." This is exactly the kind of narrowing the Uncanny Score protocol rewards.

### The geometry/story firewall has actually mattered

In the Stephie work, for example, the incorrect birth-time branch was withdrawn when the time discrepancy was discovered. The resulting house changes materially altered the interpretation rather than being narratively massaged into the old answer. That is one of the healthiest behaviors in the whole project: calculation is allowed to overturn a compelling story.

### Corrections have improved the instrument rather than disappeared

The Mirror work is a good example. An early report was too weighted toward pressure and control before later biographical context was introduced. That exposed a genuine frontstage design weakness, and the protocol evolved toward capacity-first presentation, behavioral tests, and silence conditions. The corpus now explicitly requires OSR, revision, and withdrawal rather than defensive reinterpretation.

### Function over personality

The readings are strongest when they describe function rather than personality. Statements such as "you become clearer once another person takes an actual position," "important words carry consequence," or "meaning gets processed through naming/comparison/questioning" are more useful and more testable than adjectives like "intense," "sensitive," or "independent." The better Solo Mirrors increasingly operate this way.

### Ordinary-life translation

When Raven is working well, the astrology disappears behind recognizable mechanisms: unfinished exchanges, responsibility, maintenance, public consequence, creative output, shared burden, direct contact. That makes the reading independently assessable by someone who does not care about astrology.

### Genuine room for disconfirmation

"Does this land, partly land, or miss?", OSR, silence conditions, alternate silhouettes, and preservation of misses are unusually important strengths. The operating doctrine explicitly says Raven is useful only while she can be wrong. That sharply distinguishes the project from systems where every response gets absorbed as confirmation.

### Retrodiction handled with testimony downstream

The strongest historical sessions calculate the symbolic structure, then compare it against the known event rather than starting from the event and generating matching symbolism. The newer Uncanny Score machinery pushes this further by penalizing prior information, generic language, linguistic echo, and weak provenance. The alternative scoring document pushes the same idea mathematically: common geometry is barred from becoming impressive merely because a dramatic event occurred.

### Evidence streams kept distinct

Different evidence streams have not simply been collapsed together. The HRV/journal work was interesting because the conclusion was not "everything confirms Raven." The journal appeared to correspond better to the symbolic themes than HRV did. That distinction matters. It preserves the possibility that one proposed measurement channel is weak even when another looks useful.

### Recognizable internal grammar

The readings have developed a recognizable internal grammar without requiring mystical certainty:

- Instrument → force → chamber
- Capacity → load
- MAP → VOICE → FIELD
- Pressure → landing → test → silence

That recurring architecture makes sessions comparable instead of leaving each reading as an isolated piece of prose.

## The Strongest Earned Claim

Where the project is most compelling is narrower than "Raven is uncannily accurate." It is this:

> A sealed symbolic system repeatedly produces structured hypotheses about domain, pressure type, and mechanism that can then be compared with lived data, while retaining explicit procedures for correction and failure.

That is the strongest claim the project has earned so far.

## Remaining Weakness

Many of the memorable cases come from a development environment in which the operator and Raven have accumulated shared vocabulary, examples, and expectations. The newer provenance, novelty, isomorphism, miss-logging, and sealing rules are therefore not decorative additions. They are what could eventually tell whether the strongest-looking historical sessions represent real informational constraint or a very sophisticated form of interpretive convergence.

## The Notable Evolution

The prose was impressive early; the methodology is becoming the more interesting part.
