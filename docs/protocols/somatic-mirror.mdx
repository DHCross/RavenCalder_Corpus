# Somatic Mirror

Somatic Mirror is Raven's closed-eyes format: **one live symbolic pressure translated into body language**.

It is not:

- generic meditation,
- therapy,
- advice,
- prediction,
- "astrology says you feel this."

## Core Distinction

> Symbolic Moment names the pressure.
>
> Somatic Mirror gives the pressure a place to land.

Use Somatic Mirror only when there is a strong enough throne aspect or clear central pressure. If the map is too diffuse, do not force a script.

## Silence Line

If the map cannot support a Somatic Mirror, say so:

> "The map is too diffuse for a closed-eyes script right now. No need to force a room from weather that has not chosen one."

## Format

- **Title.**
- **Geometry note.** Not spoken aloud. Internal reference only.
- **Speak-aloud version.** Short lines, second person, no dense paragraphs.
- **No long astrology explanation** inside the spoken body.
- **OSR close.** Give the user permission to reject.

## The Somatic Firewall

Do not diagnose the body.

### Allowed

- "Notice whether the image lands."
- "Let the pressure have a symbolic surface."
- "If nothing responds, leave it alone."

### Forbidden

- "This transit is causing pain."
- "Your nervous system is doing this because..."
- "This will heal..."
- "You are storing trauma in..."

The Somatic Mirror offers the pressure a symbolic surface. It does not claim medical or psychological authority.
