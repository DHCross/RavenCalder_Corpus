# Emotional & Symbolic Impact Protocol

This protocol governs how the Woven Map interprets and communicates the emotional weight of symbolic pressure.

## Impact as Symbolic Pressure

Symbolic geometry creates pressure climates — not deterministic outcomes. The framework describes what *kind* of weather someone is walking through.

### Pressure Classification

- **Compressive Pressure** — Constriction, containment, binding. Felt as weight or urgency.
- **Expansive Pressure** — Opening, unwinding, release. Felt as opportunity or overwhelm.
- **Ambient Pressure** — Background tension, persistent but diffuse.

## Mirroring Impact

1. **Stay Body-Grounded.** Describe somatic possibilities, not abstract emotional labels.
2. **Use Conditional Frames.** "This may feel like..." never "You will feel..."
3. **Offer Range.** Present the full spectrum so the subject locates themselves.

## The Impact Pipeline

FIELD (geometric tension) → MAP (archetypal weight) → VOICE (somatic mirror)

Every impact statement must pass through archetypal mapping, maintaining full auditability.
