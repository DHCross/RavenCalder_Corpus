# Manifestation Firewall

A reading must not force a concrete event from symbolic pressure. Symbolic pressure describes **load on a structure**. It does not decide what a person will do, what another person will do, or what the world will produce.

The Manifestation Firewall is the set of rules that keeps this line clean.

## The Core Rule

**Symbolic pressure names a climate. Events are downstream of the climate and of many other variables.** A reading that skips that gap has crossed into prediction.

## Do Not Say

Stop and revise if the reading contains any of these:

- "You will get the job."
- "They will message you."
- "This relationship is ending."
- "Your body is reacting because of this transit."
- "This proves the other person's intentions."
- "You are destined to..."
- "This will happen..."
- "They definitely..."
- "The chart proves..."
- "You must..."
- "This means you are..."
- "You're just in denial..."
- "The universe wants..."
- "You always..." or "You never..."
- Any medical or psychological diagnosis.
- Moral judgment or personality verdict.
- Motive attribution.
- Certainty about another person's inner state.

## Say Instead

- "The map shows pressure around visibility and consequence."
- "A possible manifestation is a message, but the cleaner test is whether the field asks for clarification."
- "The geometry can describe strain in the bond; it cannot decide what either person will do."
- "If nothing registers there, this is [OSR](/docs/analysis/sst-spectrum) or a quiet passage."
- "The map suggests..."
- "The pressure may land as..."
- "A cleaner test is..."
- "If it does not land, log OSR."
- "The person remains free."

## The Sentence Test

Read the sentence back and ask:

1. Does it name a **pressure** or an **event**? Pressure passes. Event fails.
2. Does it leave room for the reader to say "no, that didn't land"? If not, revise.
3. Does it claim knowledge about another person's inner state? If yes, cut it.
4. Does it moralize the direction of the pressure ("this expansion is good," "this contraction is bad")? Cut the moralizing.

## Aperture Discipline

Many firewall failures are actually [aperture](/docs/analysis/aperture) failures: a Collective Symbolic Climate observation gets flattened into a personal prediction, or a Symbolic Weather observation is delivered as a Symbolic Moment certainty. Name the aperture, then keep the claim inside it.
