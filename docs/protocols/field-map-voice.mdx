# FIELD → MAP → VOICE Protocol

The FIELD → MAP → VOICE protocol is the central translation pipeline of the Woven Map.

## The Three Layers

### FIELD — Raw Geometry

Computed by **Math Brain**: planetary positions, aspects, house placements, transit geometry. Pure computation — no interpretation.

### MAP — Archetypal Structure

Structured by **Raven Calder (Poetic Brain)**: maps geometric patterns to archetypal motifs, identifies tension patterns and polarity hooks.

### VOICE — Diagnostic Mirror

Delivered by **Poetic Brain**: plain-language mirrors, conditional and body-grounded, testable and falsifiable.

## Pipeline Constraints

1. Nothing reaches VOICE without anchoring in FIELD and MAP.
2. Every metaphor traceable to source geometry.
3. VOICE prefers active-process language and avoids identity verdicts. Conditional forms (_may_, _might_, _could_) are the default; strict E-Prime is optional, not a hard lint rule.

## Audit Trail

Any VOICE output can be traced back through MAP to its originating FIELD geometry. This ensures the system remains honest and corrigible.
