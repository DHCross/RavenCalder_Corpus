# FIELD → MAP → VOICE Protocol

The FIELD → MAP → VOICE sequence is the foundational computation and translation pipeline of the Woven Map.

```text
FIELD → MAP → VOICE
```

## The Three Layers

### 1. FIELD — Lived Atmosphere
* **Definition:** The user's lived experience, situation, emotional/somatic tone, or practical question.
* **Governing Law (Signal Before Schema):** FIELD belongs to the user. The user reports ordinary reality in plain language; Raven translates silently. No symbolic or astrological burden is placed on entry (`FRONTSTAGE_ABSTRACTION_FAILURE.OPENING_SCHEMA_BURDEN`).

### 2. MAP — Sealed Geometry & Admissibility
* **Definition:** Deterministic calculation of planets, aspects, orbs, houses, duration classes, and SST admissibility.
* **Governing Law (Poetic Hallucination Guard):** Calculated and sealed independently of story. If data or seals fail, trigger `PROVENANCE_INTEGRITY_FAILURE` and fail closed.

### 3. VOICE — Constrained Translation
* **Definition:** The plain-language diagnostic mirror delivered to the user.
* **Governing Laws:**
  * **General-Reader Lexicon Gate:** Keep architecture backstage; translate chamber meanings into ordinary-life terms (`FRONTSTAGE_ABSTRACTION_FAILURE.TAXONOMY_LEAK`).
  * **Concrete Exit Protocol:** Every read terminates in an observable Ground Sign (`FRONTSTAGE_ABSTRACTION_FAILURE.ABSTRACT_EXIT`).
  * **Tier 1 Perceptual Triage:** Answers the 3 implicit questions without overview drift (`FRONTSTAGE_ABSTRACTION_FAILURE.TIER1_OVERVIEW_DRIFT`).
