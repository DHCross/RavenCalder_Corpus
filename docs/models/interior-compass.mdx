# The Interior Compass

MBTI is used within the Woven Map as **symbolic vocabulary for internal cognitive motion**. It is not identity, behavior, persona, warmth, charisma, sociability, reserve, public style, or performance.

Only the **Interior Compass** may speak to type. Everything else is presentation.

## Allowed Type Sources

Only these may determine the symbolic type call:

- **Moon** as somatic engine and emotional intake.
- **Saturn** as load frame, structure-bias, and motion direction.
- **MC/IC** as purpose tone (not public identity).
- **Moon–Venus–Saturn** relational core.
- **4th, 8th, and 12th chambers** as internal processing.
- **Sun** element and modality only.
- **Mercury** element only.

## Forbidden Type Sources

These must not determine type. They belong to presentation and social interface, not to internal cognitive motion:

- Ascendant.
- Sun expression.
- Mars ignition.
- Mercury tempo.
- Social houses.
- Public style.
- Charisma.
- Relational performance.
- How others experience the person.

## The Extrovert Trap

Do not confuse visible expressiveness, social adaptation, or high contact bandwidth with baseline processing architecture.

- A person can be socially fluent and internally inward-first.
- A person can be quiet and not inward-first.

**Contact resonance may describe presentation. It may not determine type.**

The Extrovert Trap is the single most common source of MBTI miscalls in astrological reading: reading a bright Ascendant or a Mars-forward chart as an extrovert type, when the Interior Compass may point the other direction entirely.

## Five-Layer Typology Taxonomy

Use this taxonomy when the calculated engine, self-report, and visible interface diverge. Only the first layer determines type.

### 1. Interior Compass

Sealed architecture and circuit constants. **The only layer allowed to determine the symbolic type call.**

### 2. Social Canopy and Interface Gates

Contact resonance, presentation layer, social bandwidth, visible adaptation. Describes how signal crosses contact. **Does not determine type.**

### 3. External Layer

Self-report as active interface and present identification. Real lived recognition, not an error, but not type-determining.

### 4. Legacy State

Older outputs, deprecated identifications, or historical resonance. Meaningful as history but not controlling current sealed math.

### 5. Real-Time Delta

Live calibration where current symbolic pressure tests the vessel. Useful for validation, not for rewriting sealed natal geometry.

## Circuit Constants

Use circuit terms only in doctrine or technical mode unless the user asks. Backstage math may use these; frontstage language should stay human.

### Resistance (Thinking/Feeling)

Logical gating versus direct absorption. Do not moralize either side.

### Capacitance (Judging/Perceiving)

Stored, organized charge versus open-loop flow. Do not reduce to neatness or spontaneity.

### Inductance (Introversion/Extroversion)

How impact becomes outward navigation. Do not reduce to sociability.

## Why This Discipline Matters

The [Carlson study](/docs/Part_IV_The_Evidence) and its successors demonstrated that astrologers cannot reliably predict personality traits from a chart. The Interior Compass discipline is the Woven Map's response: it explicitly narrows what a chart is claiming to speak to, and it isolates the layers that presentation can reach without letting them override the sealed architecture.

The cost of ignoring this discipline is exactly the failure mode Carlson exposed: confidently reading the musician when the chart only describes the instrument.
