# The Symbolic Resonance Protocol

The Symbolic Resonance Protocol governs how the Woven Map matches symbolic patterns to lived experience.

## Resonance Conditions

1. **Geometric Presence** — The pattern must exist in chart geometry (FIELD).
2. **Archetypal Coherence** — The pattern must map to a recognizable motif (MAP).
3. **Lived Recognition** — The subject must confirm resonance (VOICE verification).

## Resonance Types

- **Direct Resonance** — Clean mapping: geometry → archetype → experience. Logged as WB.
- **Inverted Resonance** — Pattern manifests as its opposite. Logged as ABE.
- **Delayed Resonance** — Recognition comes after the ping window. Still logged as WB.
- **Null Resonance** — No recognition despite clear geometry. Logged as OSR.

## Protocol Rules

- Resonance is never assumed, always verified.
- OSR results are as valuable as WB — they calibrate the system.
- Subjects are the final authority on their experience.
- No pattern is "real" without lived ping.
