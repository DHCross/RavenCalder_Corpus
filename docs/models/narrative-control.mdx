# A Model of Narrative Regulation Under Pressure

By Dan Cross.

## 1. The Core Claim

The diagnostic variable is not how much someone edits their story. It is **the distance between editing and admitted editing**.

That distance, the Narrative Control Gap, is the observable heart of this framework. Everything else in the model is scaffolding around it.

## 2. The Self as Narrative

The self is not a static object. It is a **narrative identity**: an evolving story constructed from unconscious drives, archetypal patterns, and lived experience. That story is the lens through which we interpret the past, experience the present, and project into the future. Data that contradicts the story registers as a threat, and narrative editing is one of the responses available.

Note the scope. Narrative control is a mechanism for defending coherence under contradiction. It is not proposed here as the primary driver of human behavior. Hunger, attachment, reward, fear, status, habit, and physiology generate plenty of behavior without routing through narrative defense.

## 3. Skills Are Morally Neutral

The same competence, held with different intent, produces very different behavior. "Shield vs. weapon" is a useful frontstage phrase, but underneath it the real diagnostic questions are: _who is the control aimed at_, and _did the target consent to being edited?_ Those two questions do more work than any severity ladder (see Section 5).

## 4. The Two Axes That Actually Hold

An earlier version of this model claimed a Degree axis (low to extreme control) and a Motive axis (shield vs. weapon), then derived a 4x2 matrix. That structure collapsed on inspection: the four "degrees" also differed by motive, so the axes were not independent. The Confessor case (strategic oversharing) broke the ladder outright, because it is high-control performance dressed as low-control disclosure.

The two axes that survive:

### Axis A. Actual Control

How much selection, editing, reframing, suppression, or rewriting is actually happening. A continuum from transparent to extreme rewriting.

Actual control is **not directly observable**. It requires independent ground truth about what happened, or a signal that leaks through the account over time.

### Axis B. Performed Control

How curated the account presents itself as being. A continuum from openly curated to convincingly raw.

Performed control **is directly observable**. It is right there in how the account frames itself.

That asymmetry matters, and drives everything in Section 6.

## 5. Target and Consent (Replacing the Old Third Dimension)

An earlier draft proposed a third axis ordered as self-coherence → social presentation → influence → coercive control. That is another ladder. It orders outwardness and harm on the same scale and re-entangles them, which was the exact critique made of the original degree/motive ladder.

Two independent variables do the work more cleanly:

### Target: at whom is the control aimed?

- **Inward.** Editing the storyteller's own understanding.
- **Outward.** Editing what another person is allowed to see or believe.

### Consent: did the target agree to being edited?

- **Consented.** The audience knows and accepts that they are receiving a curated account (a memoir, a legal filing, a therapy session with disclosed reframing).
- **Non-consenting.** The audience believes they are receiving something they are not.

The four cells produced by target × consent are meaningfully different, and they no longer track severity. A defensive discloser managing an abusive parent is aimed **outward** with **no coercion** because the target is hostile and the editor is protecting themselves. A private self-deceiver is aimed **inward** but can still damage others indirectly by then sincerely acting from the rewritten version.

"Shield" and "weapon" remain useful shorthand, but they map onto the consent question, not the target question.

## 6. The Narrative Control Gap

Define:

> **Narrative Control Gap = Actual Control − Performed Control**

This is the measurement claim, and it has a measurement problem worth stating upfront.

### What is observable, and when

- **Performed control**: observable in real time, from the account itself.
- **Actual control**: not directly observable in real time. Recoverable retrospectively when independent ground truth exists (documents, witnesses, receipts, contradicting testimony).

That makes the Gap a **retrospective forensic tool** in the strongest case.

### The live-read exception: drift across retellings

Where the Gap can be inferred without external ground truth is **across multiple retellings of the same event by the same person**. Construction leaks through drift.

- **High drift.** Details shift meaningfully between tellings. Indicates active editing.
- **Suspiciously low drift.** An emotional or complex account reproduced nearly verbatim. Indicates a **rehearsed** account, which is itself a form of high actual control.
- **Ordinary drift.** Small variations consistent with memory, without directional pattern. Low Gap.

Multi-telling comparison is the closest thing this framework has to a live measurement of actual control.

### Gap regions

- **Near zero.** Presentation matches construction. Someone with heavy actual editing who openly says "I am giving you a carefully edited account" is epistemically easier to deal with than someone doing moderate editing while presenting it as unfiltered.
- **Large positive Gap.** Heavy construction presented as spontaneous or raw. Confessor territory.
- **Negative Gap.** Performing more calculation or detachment than is actually being exercised. A distinct defensive presentation worth logging separately.

The Gap is falsifiable in a way that "the self edits to protect itself" is not. It is what makes this a claim rather than a definition.

## 7. Four Recurring Strategies

These are **recurring strategies, not personality types**. A person can be a Documentarian in a lab notebook, an Integrator telling their childhood story, and a Chameleon during a job interview. Under extreme threat the same person might become Architect-like. Context and pressure are inside the model.

### The Documentarian

Low actual control, low performed control, small Gap. Lets experience speak, including material that reflects badly on themselves.

Consented form: transparent self-report. Non-consenting form: weaponized bluntness used to injure under cover of "just being honest."

### The Integrator

Moderate actual control, moderate performed control, small Gap. Refines truth into a coherent account without discarding contradictory material.

Consented form: honest sense-making, offered as sense-making. Non-consenting form: airtight self-justification presented as neutral reasoning.

### The Chameleon

High actual control, variable performed control. When actual and performed control match, this reads as visible social fluency. When performed control drops well below actual (**large positive Gap**), it becomes strategic vulnerability: high control cosplaying as low. This is the Confessor case.

Consented form: adaptive presentation the audience understands is adaptive. Non-consenting form: manipulation through curated intimacy.

### The Reality Architect

Extreme actual control. Rewrites events, not just their framing.

Inward form: rewriting one's own memory to keep the self coherent under damage. Outward non-consenting form: rewriting other people's reality to trap them in the rewritten story. Gaslighting sits in this cell.

## 8. Diagnostic Questions

- What is the estimated Narrative Control Gap? On what evidence?
- Is the Gap inferred live (from drift across retellings) or retrospectively (from independent ground truth)?
- Target: inward or outward?
- Consent: does the audience know they are receiving a curated account?
- Under pressure, what gets rewritten first: memory, meaning, or motive?
- Does the strategy hold across contexts, or does it shift with audience and stakes?

## Relationship to the Chart (Explicitly Untested)

A static natal chart cannot map context-variable behavior. Job-interview Chameleon and lab-notebook Documentarian are the same person with the same chart. The most a natal read can honestly claim is a **default posture under pressure**, and even that is a hypothesis until prospective logging supports it.

Transit-based reading has at least this advantage over static natal reading: transits move, so they can in principle track context and pressure that the natal chart cannot.

Either way, the astrological correspondences sometimes attached to this theory (**Ascendant as curated interface, Mercury as narrator, Mars as enforcer, Saturn as walls / high control, Pluto as survival editor, mutable equals permeable**, and related modality/control mappings) are **quarantined**: conventions, not derivations, not validated within this framework, and explicitly demoted to hypothesis-only in the correspondence registry. Bolting them on would reintroduce the trait-prediction failure mode the rest of the corpus is built to avoid (see [Part IV: The Evidence](/docs/Part_IV_The_Evidence)). Any older reference material that still presents these mappings as instructions should be read as historical, not doctrinal.

If a chart mapping earns its place, it will do so the same way any other Woven Map instrument earns its place: through prospective logging, OSR entries, and comparison against the falsifiability protocol described in [SST & Symbolic Spectrum](/docs/analysis/sst-spectrum), [Case Studies](/docs/validation/case-studies), and [Project Assessment](/docs/validation/project-assessment).

Until then, treat the astrological layer as a **candidate protocol under test**, not a result.

## Integration with the Woven Map

The model of narrative regulation stands on its own. The Woven Map's contribution is to name the pressure a person is under without dictating how their story should respond to it. Agency stays with the subject. The mirror only makes the editing visible enough to be examined, and the Gap gives it something to point at.
