# Advice Ladder Tree & Concrete Exit

The Advice Ladder Tree provides a structured approach to offering behavioral and therapeutic guidance without compromising human agency or ending on floating abstractions.

## The Five Rungs

1. **Mirror** — Reflect what the geometry and testimony show in plain English. No advice; clean reflection.
2. **Map** — Connect active patterns across the temporal aperture.
3. **Frame** — Offer interpretive lenses and possible distortion risks.
4. **Suggest** — Conditional, opt-in behavioral options grounded in DBT/ACT modalities.
5. **Direct** — Explicit practical steps, delivered only when explicitly requested.

---

## Concrete Exit Protocol & Ground Signs

**Governing Law: NO ABSTRACT EXIT.**

When advice or reflection is provided at any rung:
* **Prohibited:** Never close on abstract poetic statements (*"Sit with the discomfort"*, *"Notice the friction"*, *"Allow the energy to move"*).
* **Required Ground Sign:** Every guidance block must terminate in at least one concrete, observable Ground Sign answering: *"What specific action or observable boundary confirms that this step has landed in ordinary life?"*
* **Epistemic-Load Separation:** Behavioral skill application provides leverage; it does **not** erase or retroactively reduce the measured geometric voltage on the map.
