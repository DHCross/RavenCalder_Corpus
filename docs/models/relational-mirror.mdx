# Relational Mirror & Relational Custody

The Relational Mirror extends the Woven Map framework to interpersonal dynamics, modeling structural channels and friction pathways while strictly enforcing relational provenance.

## Universal Relational Custody Invariant

**Governing Law: EVERY RELATIONAL READ MAINTAINS SEPARATE CUSTODY.**

For every relational instrument (synastry, relational weather, field reports, Contact Translation, Mirror Flow), maintain distinct custody for:
* **Person A:** geometry, testimony, interpretation
* **Person B:** geometry, testimony, interpretation
* **Shared Relational Geometry:** static synastry contacts and licensed channel capacities
* **Unconfirmed Material:** asymmetric reports held provisionally

---

## Core Relational Rules

1. **Testimony Rule:** Person A's testimony establishes Person A's report only. It does **not** establish Person B's feelings, motives, intentions, private thoughts, or future choices (`RELATIONAL_PROVENANCE_COLLAPSE.UNATTRIBUTED_TESTIMONY_MERGE` / `CROSS_SUBJECT_STATE_INFERENCE`).
2. **Geometry Rule:** Synastry contacts describe *static structural capacity and friction channels*; they do not certify that both parties currently experience the contact identically (`STATIC_CAPACITY_AS_CURRENT_STATE`).
3. **Weather Rule:** Current transit weather is calculated separately for each person. Never derive one person's internal condition from another's weather or from static synastry.

---

## Relational Mirror Structure

* **Relational Channel Map:** Identifies shared friction channels (hard aspects) and flow channels (trines/sextiles).
* **Separate Weather Overlay:** Inspects how current atmospheric load touches Person A's anchors and Person B's anchors individually.
* **Ground Signs:** Closes on concrete, observable interaction markers (e.g. *"One sign this tension is active is a conversation stalling over who defines the timeline"*).
