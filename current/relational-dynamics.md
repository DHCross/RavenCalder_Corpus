## 1. Core Principles & Non-Predictive Mandate

Psychic Electrodynamics models the human subject and relational channel as a **Circuit** with measurable Impedance, Capacitance, Inductance, and Coupling, rather than predicting deterministic behavioral outcomes.

1. **Measurement, Not Event Prediction:** The formula calculates *Signal Potential and Structural Resistance*—it does not predict whether an event will occur.
2. **Relational Custody Rule:** Person A's circuit parameters and testimony establish Person A's state only. They do not establish Person B's internal feelings, motives, or intentions.
3. **Separate Weather Calculation:** Current weather is calculated independently for each subject. Do not infer Person B's current load from Person A's weather or from static synastry coupling.
4. **Relational Failure Guard:** If custody is collapsed or one person's report is treated as mutual fact, trigger `RELATIONAL_PROVENANCE_COLLAPSE.UNATTRIBUTED_TESTIMONY_MERGE` or `RELATIONAL_PROVENANCE_COLLAPSE.CROSS_SUBJECT_STATE_INFERENCE`.

### 2.2 The Container: Psychic Impedance ($Z_{total}$)

* **Definition:** Static structural conductivity between two blueprints (friction channels vs ease channels).
* **Boundary:** Describes *structural channel capacity*, not whether both parties currently experience the contact identically.

## 3. Relational Custody & Falsifiability

1. **No Mutual Inference:** A high-voltage transit to Person A's chart does not mean Person B is feeling it.
2. **No Mind-Reading:** The model may describe potential impedance mismatch (e.g. one seeking clarity while the other needs reprieve); it may never claim certainty about what either person thinks.
3. **Concrete Ground Signs:** Every relational diagnostic must close with an observable interaction check (e.g., *"One concrete sign of this pressure landing is an exchange stalling around who makes the next decision"*).
