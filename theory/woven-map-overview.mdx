# Woven Map Theory

The Woven Map is grounded in a specific epistemology: symbolic geometry reflects probability fields, not deterministic scripts. This section explores the theoretical foundations.

## Core Concepts

### The Map Is Not the Land

Symbols describe patterns — they do not cause events. The Woven Map holds the tension between structure (geometry) and agency (choice). Predictions are impossible; mirrors are offered instead.

### Non-Linear Time

Pressure doesn't follow a linear timeline. It accumulates in loops, returns through retrograde signatures, and crosses thresholds. The framework tracks these recursive patterns rather than assuming forward-only progression.

### Probability Fields, Not Fate

Geometric configurations create climates where certain experiences are *more or less likely* — never *inevitable*. The framework describes probability gradients, not certainties.

## Theoretical Instruments

- **Recursion Fields** — How past pressure echoes through present configurations.
- **Probabilistic Forecasting** — The epistemology of symbolic prediction.
- **Psychic Electrodynamics** — The energy model underlying symbolic tension.
- **Death Signatures** — How terminal patterns register in the Woven Map.
- **The Cultural Axis** — How collective symbolism intersects individual charts.

- All claims must be falsifiable.
- No geometry is "active" without lived recognition.
- OSR (Outside Symbolic Range) is valid, valuable data.
- The system is corrigible — when it's wrong, it learns.
