# Psychic Electrodynamics

Psychic Electrodynamics provides the energy model for how symbolic tension operates within the Woven Map framework. It describes pressure as dynamic charge — building, discharging, and transferring across symbolic circuits.

## The Charge Model

Symbolic pressure behaves analogously to electrical charge:

- **Potential Difference** — Tension between planetary positions creates symbolic voltage.
- **Current** — Active transits carry charge through the system.
- **Resistance** — Hard aspects create impedance where pressure builds.
- **Discharge** — Recognition (ping) releases accumulated tension.

## Circuit Patterns

### Closed Circuits

When tension has no outlet — no recognition, no expression — pressure builds. This maps to suppressed vectors in the Vector Architecture.

### Open Circuits

When tension flows freely — recognized, expressed, integrated — pressure dissipates. This maps to released vectors.

### Short Circuits

When pressure discharges suddenly — breakthroughs, crises, revelations — the system experiences rapid state change. These are tracked as volatility spikes on the Seismograph.

## Clinical Application

The electrodynamic model helps frame psychological experience in non-pathologizing terms. "Anxiety" becomes "accumulated charge seeking discharge." "Breakthrough" becomes "circuit completion." The model describes without diagnosing.
