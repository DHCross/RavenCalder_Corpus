# Death Signatures Theory

Death Signatures Theory explores how terminal patterns — endings, closures, and completions — register in the Woven Map's symbolic framework.

## Terminal Geometry

Certain geometric configurations correlate statistically with termination events:

- **Saturn-Pluto conjunctions** — Structural endings and rebirths.
- **8th House activations** — Transformation and closure pressure.
- **Nodal axis crossings** — Fated endings and new beginnings.
- **Chiron returns** — Wound cycles completing.

## The Distinction

Death Signatures do not predict literal death. They describe *ending climates* — periods when closure pressure is heightened. What ends may be a relationship, a career phase, an identity structure, or a life.

## Ethical Handling

- Death Signatures are never presented as predictions of literal death.
- The framework describes closure pressure, not outcomes.
- Subjects retain full agency in how they navigate ending climates.
- If the framework can't speak helpfully, it stays silent.

## Research Basis

The theory is grounded in statistical correlation between geometric patterns and documented termination events, validated through retrodictive analysis. All findings are probabilistic, not deterministic.
