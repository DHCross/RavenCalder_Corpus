# Recursion Fields

*Version: 9.8.25*

Recursion Fields describe how past pressure doesn't dissipate but echoes through present configurations. Retrograde signatures, returns, and threshold crossings create recursive loops in the symbolic landscape.

## The Nature of Recursion

Symbolic pressure is not linear. A transit doesn't simply "happen and pass." Past configurations leave resonance trails that activate when similar geometry recurs.

### Types of Recursive Patterns

- **Retrograde Echoes** — When a planet retrogrades over a previously activated degree, past themes resurface.
- **Return Signatures** — Planetary returns (Saturn, Jupiter, etc.) reactivate the natal configuration's archetypal pressure.
- **Threshold Crossings** — When a slow-moving planet crosses an angle or nodal axis, recursive patterns amplify.

## Tracking Recursion

The Woven Map tracks recursion through:

1. **Geometric Overlay** — Comparing current transits against historical activation points.
2. **Ping History** — Reviewing past SST logs for WB/ABE patterns that may reactivate.
3. **Loop Mapping** — Identifying repeating aspect patterns across time windows.

## Clinical Implications

Recursion fields explain why "old issues" resurface during certain transits. The framework doesn't pathologize this — it normalizes recursive pressure as inherent to symbolic weather, not personal regression.
