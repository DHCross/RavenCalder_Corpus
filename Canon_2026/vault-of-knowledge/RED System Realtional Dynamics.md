## **III. Technical Appendix: Computation & Scaling**

### **1. The 0–5 Normalized Scale**
All raw sums are saturated at **5.0** to prevent runaway scoring.

### **2. Computability Definitions**
* **Tension-Spark ($S_t$):** Sum of weighted Squares/Oppositions. $\text{Sat} = \min(\text{Sum}, 5.0)$.
* **Activation-Spark ($S_a$):** Sum of weighted Conjunctions. $\text{Sat} = \min(\text{Sum}, 5.0)$.
* **Total Potential ($V_p$):** $\max(S_t, S_a) + [0.5 \times \min(S_t, S_a)]$. $(\text{Max } 5.0)$.
* **Containment Damping ($D_s$):** $\sum(\text{Saturn Aspects} \times 1.0) + \sum(\text{Neptune Aspects} \times 1.0) + \min(\text{Earth Sign Count} \times 0.3, 1.5)$. Result capped at **5.0**.
* **Impedance Mismatch ($I_m$):** Mean Absolute Difference (MAD) of Circuit Constants:
  $$I_m = \frac{|R_1 - R_2| + |C_1 - C_2| + |L_1 - L_2|}{3}$$

### **3. Transmission Integrity Formula ($S$)**
$$S = \frac{V_p}{I_m + D_s + 1}$$

* **High $S$ (> 2.5):** Clear, persistent signal capacity.
* **Low $S$ (< 1.5):** Signal capacity constrained by resistance/damping.
