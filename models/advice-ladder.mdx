# Advice Ladder Tree

*Version: Dec.25*

The Advice Ladder Tree is a therapeutic integration protocol designed for relational diagnostics within the Woven Map framework. It provides a structured approach to offering guidance without compromising agency.

## The Ladder Model

Guidance is offered in tiers, escalating only with subject consent:

1. **Mirror** — Reflect what the geometry shows. No advice, just reflection.
2. **Map** — Connect patterns across time. "This resembles a pattern from [prior period]."
3. **Frame** — Offer interpretive lenses. "Some people experience this as..."
4. **Suggest** — Conditional, opt-in guidance. "If you were open to exploring..."
5. **Direct** — Clear guidance, only when explicitly requested by the subject.

## Integration Protocol

The Advice Ladder integrates with:
- **Relational Mirror** — Synastry and composite chart analysis.
- **SST Classification** — WB/ABE/OSR logging for therapeutic interventions.
- **Balance Meter** — Tracking support-friction differentials in relationships.

## Constraints

- Never climb the ladder without subject invitation.
- Every rung is optional — the subject can decline at any point.
- Mirror (Level 1) is always available and often sufficient.
- Direct guidance (Level 5) requires explicit consent.
