# A Unified Theory of Narrative Control

This model explores how narrative structures — the stories we tell ourselves — shape psychological experience. It integrates with the Woven Map by mapping narrative patterns to symbolic geometry.

## Core Thesis

The stories we inhabit are not passive reflections of experience — they actively shape what we perceive, how we interpret, and what we believe is possible. Narrative control is the capacity to recognize, interrogate, and revise these stories.

## Narrative Structures

### Constricting Narratives

- **Fixed Identity Stories** — "I am this kind of person, always have been."
- **Inevitability Stories** — "This always happens to me."
- **Victim-Perpetrator Binaries** — Collapsed complexity.

### Expansive Narratives

- **Exploratory Stories** — "I wonder what else might be true."
- **Agency Stories** — "I have choices, even in constraint."
- **Complexity Stories** — Holding multiple truths simultaneously.

## Integration with Woven Map

The Woven Map identifies symbolic pressure that may reinforce constricting narratives. By naming the geometry, the framework creates space for narrative revision — not by denying pressure, but by seeing it clearly enough to choose a response.

## Therapeutic Application

The Narrative Control model provides a language for discussing psychological patterns without pathologizing them. Stories can be held, examined, and rewritten — agency remains with the subject.
