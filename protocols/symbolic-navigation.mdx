# Symbolic Navigation

*Version: 9.8.25*

Symbolic navigation describes how the Woven Map traverses the symbolic landscape — how it identifies, prioritizes, and interprets the geometry of a chart.

## Navigation Principles

### Field-First Orientation

Navigation always begins with FIELD — raw geometry — before moving to MAP (archetypal motifs) and VOICE (plain-language mirrors). The pipeline is strictly sequential and auditable.

### Tension-Driven Routing

The framework routes attention to where tension is highest:

1. Hard aspects between personal and outer planets
2. Angular placements
3. Anaretic degrees (29°)
4. Anchor planet configurations

### Loop Awareness

Pressure doesn't unfold linearly. The framework tracks recursive loops, retrograde signatures, and threshold returns. Past pressure doesn't dissipate — it echoes.

## Navigation Instruments

- **Hook Stack** — Identifies daily hot spots where tension is most felt.
- **Seismograph** — Measures magnitude, valence, and volatility of pressure.
- **Vector Map** — Tracks containment and release patterns across tension channels.

## Relocation Handling

When a subject has relocated:
- Houses and angles move to the new location.
- Natal typology and planetary configurations remain fixed.
- Transit interpretations adapt to relocated houses.
- House claims are suppressed if birth time confidence is low.
