# FIELD → MAP → VOICE Protocol

*Version: 2025-09.8.25*

The FIELD → MAP → VOICE protocol is the central translation pipeline of the Woven Map. It ensures every output is traceable, testable, and agency-preserving.

## The Three Layers

### FIELD — Raw Geometry

Computed by **Math Brain**. This layer contains:
- Planetary positions and aspects
- House placements
- Transit geometry
- Energetic pressure windows

FIELD is pure computation — no interpretation, no narrative.

### MAP — Archetypal Structure

Structured by **Raven Calder (Poetic Brain)**. This layer:
- Maps geometric patterns to archetypal motifs
- Identifies tension patterns and polarity hooks
- Builds symbolic scaffolding from raw geometry

MAP is where meaning is structured — but still abstract, before voice.

### VOICE — Diagnostic Mirror

Delivered by **Poetic Brain**. This layer:
- Renders archetypal motifs as plain-language mirrors
- Uses conditional, body-grounded language
- Produces testable, falsifiable output

## Pipeline Constraints

1. Nothing reaches VOICE without anchoring in FIELD and MAP.
2. Every metaphor must be traceable to source geometry.
3. Metaphor stays leashed to geometry — no free association.
4. E-Prime enforced at VOICE layer: may, might, could only.

## Audit Trail

The pipeline enables full auditability: any VOICE output can be traced back through MAP to its originating FIELD geometry. This ensures the system remains honest and corrigible.
