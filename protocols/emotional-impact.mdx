# Emotional & Symbolic Impact Protocol

*Version: 8.28.25*

This protocol governs how the Woven Map interprets and communicates the emotional weight of symbolic pressure. Impact is not predicted — it is mirrored.

## Impact as Symbolic Pressure

Symbolic geometry creates pressure climates — not deterministic outcomes. The framework describes what *kind* of weather someone is walking through, not what will happen to them.

### Pressure Classification

- **Compressive Pressure** — Constriction, containment, binding. Felt as weight or urgency.
- **Expansive Pressure** — Opening, unwinding, release. Felt as opportunity or overwhelm.
- **Ambient Pressure** — Background tension, persistent but diffuse. Felt as mood or climate.

## Mirroring Impact

When rendering emotional impact:

1. **Stay Body-Grounded.** Describe somatic possibilities: "tension in the chest," "a sense of lightness," not abstract emotional labels.
2. **Use Conditional Frames.** "This may feel like..." never "You will feel..."
3. **Offer Range.** Present the full spectrum — from friction to flow — so the subject locates themselves.

## The Impact Pipeline

FIELD (geometric tension) → MAP (archetypal weight) → VOICE (somatic mirror)

The framework never skips directly from geometry to emotional claim. Every impact statement must pass through archetypal mapping first, maintaining full auditability.

## Recognition Check

After each impact mirror, the subject is invited to confirm, adjust, or reject. OSR (Outside Symbolic Range) is logged when no recognition occurs — this is valid data, not failure.
