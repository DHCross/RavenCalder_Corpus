# The Balance Meter

The Balance Meter converts raw symbolic pressure into narrative climate. It integrates four diagnostic channels to produce a holistic picture of the subject's current symbolic weather.

## The Four Channels

### 1. Seismograph
Measures the raw intensity of current pressure:
- **Magnitude (⚡):** How intense is the pressure?
- **Valence (🌞/🌑/🌗):** Is the tone bright, dark, or mixed?
- **Volatility (🌪️):** How rapidly is the pressure shifting?

### 2. Balance Channel
Assesses structural support vs. strain:
- Where is scaffolding holding?
- Where is support cutting or failing?
- Net tension distribution across life domains.

### 3. Support–Friction Differential (SFD)
Computes the net balance between:
- **Stabilizers:** Supportive transits, harmonious aspects, protective houses.
- **Strain:** Challenging transits, hard aspects, activated tension points.

### 4. Resilience Layer
Evaluates capacity for handling pressure:
- **Endurance:** How long can the subject sustain current pressure?
- **Depletion:** Where are reserves running low?
- **Rebound Potential:** Capacity for recovery after pressure peaks.

## Climate Output

All four channels are synthesized into plain-language climate descriptions: "pressure bands," "support or cut," and "reserves and recovery." The Balance Meter describes weather — never predicts storms.
