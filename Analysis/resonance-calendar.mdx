# The Resonance Activation Calendar (RAC)

The RAC maps timing windows when specific symbolic patterns are most likely to activate. It integrates transit tracking, progression analysis, and historical ping data to forecast resonance peaks.

## Calendar Construction

### Input Data
- Current and upcoming transits
- Progressed chart movements
- Historical ping logs (WB/ABE patterns)
- Seasonal and cyclical patterns

### Output
- **Activation Windows:** Date ranges when specific archetypes are most active.
- **Peak Days:** High-probability dates for strong resonance.
- **Quiet Periods:** Intervals with minimal symbolic activation.
- **Echo Windows:** Periods when past patterns may resurface.

## Usage

The RAC is used for:
- **Session Planning:** Scheduling readings during high-resonance windows.
- **Longitudinal Tracking:** Correlating calendar predictions with actual pings.
- **Pattern Research:** Identifying cyclical regularities in symbolic activation.

## Constraints

- Activation windows are probabilistic, not deterministic.
- The calendar describes likelihoods, never certainties.
- Historical ping data improves accuracy over time.
- OSR logging during predicted peaks provides valuable calibration data.
